WHY THE BOX ZONE
Cycling lives in the left 46px — a deliberate reach, so a palm brush on the row body never changes state. Body tap = item detail. Hold = everything else. Cycle stops at BOUGHT and locks for 600ms.
DENSITY MATH
54px simple rows, ~74px noted rows → 9–10 items per screenful at 390×844. A 25-item store fits in ~2.5 thumbscrolls, sorted by aisle so the scroll order is the walk order.
STALE ≠ BROKEN
Poor signal is normal on the Saddle. Stale data = amber timestamp. Queued change = dashed clock chip on the row. No spinners, no blocking, no red.
